This page is provided for information. Contact jeff@gearedforgreen.com with any questions.

Accepted materials

Boxes are for accepted materials only. Do not put these items in a Circular Express Box:

  • Food waste
  • Liquids
  • Glass
  • Batteries
  • Electronics
  • Hazardous materials

Contaminated shipments may forfeit points.

Shipping

Prepaid labels cover the box you purchased. Shipping is available in the United States only.

Rewards points

Points have no cash value. Points cannot be transferred outside a team program.

Boxes and returns

Boxes are consumable. Once a box has been used, it is not returnable.

Questions

Questions about these terms go to jeff@gearedforgreen.com.
